## What Is FREEship

FREEship is a Windows hull modeler aimed at people who already intend to cut a small boat. You pull a control net, watch stations, and ask FREEship hydrostatic calc whether the canoe body matches the brief on your notepad. FREEship hull modeler is the surface. FREEship offset table is the lofting handoff. FREEship small craft is the honest scale: kayaks, daysailers, launches, fishing skiffs you will stitch, strip, or plywood yourself.

It exists because not every hull belongs in MAXSURF, and not every builder wants a production CAD. The program still demands fairing discipline. A hollow in the bow will show up in plywood as surely as it would in a paid suite. Students in a club you administer and home builders with a set of plans they have the right to use are the usual seats.

Draw hulls you designed or were given permission to develop. Do not load a commercial offsets table you do not own. Keep versions before each weekend of lofting.

## Starting with FREEship

Prerequisites: A PC you administer, a brief for a small boat you plan to loft, and dimensions you are allowed to use.

1. **Prepare:** Write target length, beam, and displacement on paper beside the keyboard.
2. **Configure:** Start FREEship hull modeler from a simple parent close to that brief.
3. **Test:** Move a few points and confirm the stations still look like a skiff, not a crumpled bag.
4. **Refine:** Fair until buttocks are calm, then run FREEship hydrostatic calc against the notepad brief.
5. **Apply:** Export FREEship offset table and loft on the floor you actually have.
6. **Maintain:** Save a copy before each cutting weekend, and do not mix two boats in one file.
